# Holiday Shipping Update 2026

This update applies from November 20, 2026 to December 31, 2026 and temporarily changes parts
of the regular shipping policy. After December 31, 2026 the regular shipping policy applies
again.

## Free shipping threshold

During the holiday period, standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more within the
contiguous United States (the regular threshold is $75).

## Order-by dates for delivery before December 24

- Standard shipping: order by December 16, 2026.
- Expedited shipping: order by December 20, 2026.
- Overnight shipping: order by 1 p.m. Mountain Time on December 22, 2026.

## Extended holiday returns

Items purchased between November 20 and December 24, 2026 can be returned until January 31,
2027, even if the normal return window ends earlier.

## Customer care hours

Customer care chat is open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Mountain Time every day from December 1 to
December 23, 2026. Customer care is closed on December 25, 2026.

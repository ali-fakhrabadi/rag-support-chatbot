LARKSPUR OUTFITTERS - STORES, HOURS AND SERVICES

Boise flagship store
Monday to Saturday: 9 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Sunday: 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
Services: in-store pickup, gear rentals, ski and snowboard tuning.

Salt Lake City store
Monday to Saturday: 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Sunday: 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.
Services: in-store pickup.

Bend store
Open every day: 9 a.m. to 7 p.m.
Services: in-store pickup, gear rentals.

Holidays
All stores are closed on Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

In-store pickup
Online orders marked "pickup" are usually ready within 2 hours during store opening hours.
Orders are held at the store for 7 days; after that they are cancelled and refunded.
Bring the order confirmation and a photo ID.

Gear rentals (Boise and Bend stores only)
- 2-person tent: $24 per day
- sleeping bag: $12 per day
- snowshoes: $15 per day
Rentals require a $100 refundable security deposit. Reservations can be made up to 60 days in
advance. Rental gear returned late is charged one extra day for each day it is late.

Ski and snowboard tuning (Boise only)
A basic tune costs $35 and is ready within 48 hours.

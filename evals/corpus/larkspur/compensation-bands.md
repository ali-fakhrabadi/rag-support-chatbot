# Compensation Bands 2026 (Confidential - HR and leadership only)

## Retail

- Store associate: $17 to $21 per hour.
- Assistant store manager: $52,000 to $61,000 per year.
- Store manager: $68,000 to $84,000 per year.

## Headquarters

- Customer care specialist: $44,000 to $52,000 per year.
- Software engineer II: $118,000 to $142,000 per year.
- Senior product designer: $112,000 to $134,000 per year.

## Review cycle

Salary bands are reviewed every year in February. Merit increases take effect on April 1.

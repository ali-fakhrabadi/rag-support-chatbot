LARKSPUR CLUB - MEMBER GUIDE

Joining
Larkspur Club is free to join online or in any store. Members need an email address and must be
at least 18 years old.

Earning points
Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on eligible purchases and 2 points for every $1 spent on
Larkspur-brand items. Points are not earned on gift card purchases, shipping fees or taxes.
Points from an order are added to the account 14 days after the order ships, so that returns can
be taken into account.

Rewards
Every 500 points convert automatically into a $25 reward. Rewards can be used online and in
stores, and expire 90 days after they are issued. Points expire if an account has no purchase or
reward activity for 18 months.

Tiers
All members start in the Trailblazer tier. Members who spend $1,000 or more in a calendar year
reach the Summit tier for the rest of that year and the whole following year.

Summit tier benefits:
- free expedited shipping on every order,
- a 15% birthday discount on one order during the birthday month,
- early access to seasonal sales.

Member returns
Larkspur Club members have a 60-day return window and free return shipping on online orders.

Account changes
Members can close their account at any time. Unused points and rewards are forfeited when an
account is closed and cannot be transferred to another member.

# Returns and Refunds Policy

Larkspur Outfitters wants every customer to be happy with their gear. This policy applies to
purchases made on larkspur.example and in Larkspur retail stores in the United States.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 45 days of the delivery date (or the purchase date for
in-store purchases). Larkspur Club members get an extended return window of 60 days.

Items must be unused, clean and in their original packaging with tags attached. Footwear that
has been worn outdoors cannot be returned unless it is defective; defective items are handled
under the Larkspur Gear Warranty instead of this policy.

## Items that cannot be returned

The following items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

- clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the product page or price tag,
- gift cards and e-gift cards,
- custom-embroidered or personalized items,
- opened fuel canisters and opened bear spray.

## How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. We process the refund within 5 business days
after the returned item is inspected at our Boise warehouse. Card issuers can take up to 10
additional business days to show the credit on a statement.

Returns without a receipt or order number receive store credit for the lowest selling price of
the item in the last 90 days. Items received as gifts are refunded as store credit to the gift
recipient, never to the original purchaser.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free for Larkspur Club members and for items that arrived damaged or
defective. For all other online returns, a prepaid return label costs $7.95, which is deducted
from the refund. Returning an online order to any Larkspur store is always free.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color of the same item ship free of charge. An exchange must
be requested within the same return window that applies to a return. If the replacement costs
more, the difference is charged to the original payment method; if it costs less, the
difference is refunded.

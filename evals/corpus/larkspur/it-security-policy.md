# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Use the company-provided password manager for every work account.
- Do not reuse any of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords are not rotated on a schedule; change a password immediately if it may have been
  exposed.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company system. Administrators and
  finance staff must use a hardware security key as their second factor.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the security team within 1 hour of noticing, by
calling the IT help line at extension 4357 or emailing security@larkspur.example. The device
will be locked and wiped remotely.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the "Report Phish" button in the email client. Do not forward
suspicious emails to colleagues and do not click links or open attachments in them.

## Laptops

Company laptops use full-disk encryption. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of
inactivity. Operating system security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops.

## Data classification

Larkspur uses four data classes: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Customer payment
card data is Restricted: it must never be stored in shared drives, email or chat, and may only
be processed in the payment system.

## Security training

All employees complete security awareness training every year by January 31. New hires complete
it within their first 14 days.

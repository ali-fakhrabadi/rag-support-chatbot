VENDOR ONBOARDING NOTES (PROCUREMENT)

Payment terms
Standard payment terms for approved vendors are net 45 days from the invoice date. Invoices must
include the purchase order number, otherwise they are returned to the vendor unpaid.

Insurance
Every vendor that works on Larkspur premises must provide a certificate of insurance showing at
least $2 million in general liability coverage before the first visit.

Onboarding time
Vendor onboarding takes 10 business days after procurement receives the completed vendor form,
the tax form and the certificate of insurance.

# Larkspur Gear Warranty

## The Trail Promise

Every Larkspur-brand product is covered by the Trail Promise, a lifetime warranty against
defects in materials and workmanship. "Lifetime" means the functional life of the product, not
the life of the owner.

The Trail Promise does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, damage from improper care or
storage, alterations, or damage caused by animals. See the Product Care Guide for how to look
after your gear.

## Electronics

Larkspur-brand electronics, such as headlamps, GPS units and power banks, are covered for 2 years
from the purchase date instead of a lifetime.

## Other brands

Products from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty. Larkspur helps
customers file those claims during the first 12 months after purchase.

## How to file a warranty claim

File a claim with the online warranty form. Include the order number (or the store receipt) and
clear photos of the problem. Claims are reviewed within 10 business days.

If the claim is approved, Larkspur will repair the item, replace it with the same or a similar
item, or issue store credit for the current price of the item. Larkspur chooses the remedy.

## Repairs

Repairs are done at the Larkspur Repair Center in Ogden, Utah. Repair turnaround is usually 3 to
4 weeks, plus shipping time. Larkspur pays shipping both ways for approved warranty repairs.

Items that are not covered can still be repaired for a fee. The repair center sends a quote
before starting paid work; quotes are valid for 30 days.

## Summit Care extended protection

Summit Care is an optional protection plan that covers accidental damage, which the Trail Promise
does not cover. Examples are rips and tears, broken tent poles, burn holes and zippers broken in
an accident.

- Price: 12% of the item's purchase price.
- Duration: 3 years from the purchase date.
- Deductible: none.
- Limit: up to 2 claims per 12 months.

Summit Care must be purchased within 30 days of buying the item it protects. It is
non-transferable and can be cancelled within the first 30 days for a full refund. Summit Care is
not available for electronics, footwear or clearance items.

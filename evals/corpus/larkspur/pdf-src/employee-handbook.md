# Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Working hours

The standard work week for full-time employees is 40 hours. Office-based employees are expected
to be available during core hours, 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Mountain Time, Monday to Friday. Retail
schedules are published two weeks in advance.

## Remote work

Office-based employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's approval.
Retail and warehouse roles are not eligible for remote work. Employees working remotely must
use the company VPN and follow the Information Security Policy.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per year during their first 3 years
of employment, and 23 days per year from the start of their fourth year. PTO accrues every pay
period. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next calendar year; any remaining
days are forfeited.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Full-time employees receive 8 paid sick days per year. A
doctor's note is only required for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

After 6 months of employment, primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave
and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken
within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Employee discount

Employees receive a 40% discount on Larkspur-brand items and a 20% discount on other brands. The
discount applies to purchases of up to $2,500 per calendar year and is for personal use and
immediate family only. Reselling discounted items is grounds for termination.

## Gear testing program

Employees can borrow demo gear from the gear library for up to 14 days at a time to test it
before recommending it to customers. Damaged gear must be reported when it is returned.

## Volunteering

Every employee receives 2 paid volunteer days per year to support conservation and community
organizations.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in March and September. Promotions are announced
after the September review.

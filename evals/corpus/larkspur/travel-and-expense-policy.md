# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all Larkspur Outfitters employees who travel or spend money on behalf of
the company.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expense reports in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required
for every expense over $25. Managers approve or reject reports within 5 business days, and
approved expenses are reimbursed in the next regular payroll.

## Approvals before travel

Any trip with an estimated total cost over $3,000 needs approval from a vice president before
booking. Trips under that amount need approval from the employee's manager only.

## Air travel

Book flights at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Economy class is required for
flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class
is not reimbursed.

## Lodging

The nightly lodging limit is $210 before taxes. In high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco,
Seattle and Boston) the limit is $275 per night.

## Meals

Meals are reimbursed with a per diem of $65 per day for domestic travel and $85 per day for
international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Ground transportation

Employees who use a personal car for business are reimbursed $0.67 per mile. Rental cars should
be compact or mid-size; ride-sharing and taxis are fine for trips between the airport and the
hotel.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year can request a corporate card. Corporate cards
must be used for any single travel purchase over $500. The card statement must be reconciled in
the expense tool within 25 days of the statement date.

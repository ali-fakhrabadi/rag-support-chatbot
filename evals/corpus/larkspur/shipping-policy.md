# Shipping Policy

## Processing time

Orders ship from our warehouse in Boise, Idaho. Orders placed before 1 p.m. Mountain Time on a
business day are processed the same day; other orders are processed within 1 business day.
You can change the shipping address within 1 hour of placing the order by contacting customer
care. After that, the order cannot be changed.

## Shipping options within the contiguous United States

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3 to 6 business days | Free on orders of $75 or more, otherwise $6.95 |
| Expedited | 2 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | next business day | $29.95 |

Overnight orders must be placed before 1 p.m. Mountain Time to ship the same day.

## Alaska, Hawaii and Canada

Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by standard service only, for a flat $19.95, and arrive in 7 to
12 business days. Overnight shipping is not available to Alaska or Hawaii.

Larkspur ships internationally to Canada only. Canadian orders cost a flat $24.95, arrive in 7
to 14 business days, and the customer pays any duties and taxes on delivery.

## Restricted items

Fuel canisters and bear spray are hazardous materials. They ship by ground service only and
cannot be shipped to Alaska, Hawaii or Canada.

## Lost or damaged packages

Report a lost package within 30 days of the expected delivery date. Larkspur files the claim
with the carrier, which usually takes up to 8 business days, and then sends a replacement or a
refund. Report damaged items within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item and the box.

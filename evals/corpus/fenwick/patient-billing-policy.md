# Patient Billing Policy

Fenwick Dental Group bills insurance first and then the patient for any remaining balance.

## Payment

Patients pay their estimated share at the time of the visit. Statements are sent within 10 days
after the insurance company pays its part.

## Refunds of overpayments

If a patient has paid more than they owe, Fenwick refunds the overpayment within 30 days after
the insurance claim is closed.

## Missed appointments

Appointments cancelled less than 24 hours in advance, and missed appointments, are charged a $50
fee. The fee is waived once per patient per year.

## Payment plans

Balances over $500 can be paid in monthly installments over up to 12 months with no interest.

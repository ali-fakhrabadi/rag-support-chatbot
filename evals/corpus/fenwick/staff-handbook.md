# Staff Handbook

## Time off

Full-time staff receive 15 days of paid time off per year. Requests must be submitted at least
4 weeks in advance because the schedule is planned monthly.

## Uniforms

Each clinical staff member receives a scrubs allowance of $300 per year.

## Infection control

Sterilizer logs are kept for 7 years. Every autoclave cycle is recorded with the date, cycle
number and the initials of the staff member who ran it.

## Continuing education

Fenwick pays up to $1,200 per year for continuing education courses for dental hygienists.
